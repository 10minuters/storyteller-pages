StoryTeller uploader

StoryTeller makes short, hand-drawn true-story videos: lucky inventions, surprising maps, how machines work, odd history and animals. The videos are published on YouTube at @StoryTellerSpeaking.

The StoryTeller uploader is a personal desktop tool used only by the channel's owner. It sends the owner's own finished videos from the owner's computer to the owner's own social media accounts (YouTube, Instagram and TikTok) through the official APIs of those platforms. On TikTok it places videos in the owner's drafts; the owner reviews and posts each one in the TikTok app.

The tool is not offered to other users.
